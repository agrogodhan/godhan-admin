Breeds

The one breed list used by the app (Add Cattle, lineage, calving, insemination), the bull catalog and the breed mix. Pick a breed's aliases for other spellings people use — they are converted to the breed's name automatically. A breed's name and type can't be changed after it is created; deactivate it instead to hide it from the app's lists.
